About Us

Who

Kitchen 96 is a family-owned catering business based in the Roxbury area of Boston, Massachusetts, specializing in delicious Haitian and Caribbean cuisine. The business was created with a passion for bringing authentic Caribbean flavors to the local community while providing food that feels homemade and comforting. Kitchen 96 offers a variety of flavorful dishes inspired by traditional Haitian and Caribbean recipes, using quality ingredients and plenty of care in every meal.

What

What makes Kitchen 96 special is the family-centered approach behind the business. Everyone plays a role in preparing, organizing, and serving the food, creating an environment where cooking is a shared experience. The menu features a variety of Caribbean favorites, including seasoned meats, rice and beans, stews, fried plantains, and other traditional sides. Each dish reflects the bold flavors and cultural traditions that make Haitian and Caribbean food unique.

Where

Kitchen 96 is proud to serve the Roxbury community and the greater Boston area for family gatherings, celebrations, parties, community events, and other special occasions. Whether customers are looking for a full catering service or simply want to enjoy a flavorful homemade meal, Kitchen 96 aims to make every experience memorable. Through its food, the business hopes to bring people together while celebrating the culture, traditions, and community that inspire its cooking.
